## What We're Building

A mobile-friendly website that does two things simultaneously — builds your brand and captures real customers from day one.

When cafe visitors scan the QR code on their table, they land on a simple, beautiful page that introduces TrueBites, offers them a free sattu drink, and collects their details. After they try the drink, they share quick feedback and walk away with a discount on your makhanas. You walk away with a real lead, a genuine product review, and a warm potential customer on WhatsApp.

The website also comes with a private dashboard so you can track signups, read feedback, and manage cafe partners — all in one place.

---

## How the Customer Journey Works

```
Customer scans QR code at cafe table
            ↓
Lands on your campaign page (on their phone)
            ↓
Enters their name + WhatsApp number
            ↓
Receives a unique code on screen
            ↓
Shows code to cafe staff → gets free sattu drink
            ↓
Fills a quick 30-second feedback form
            ↓
Receives 30% off on makhanas
+ one-tap button to place order on WhatsApp
```

Every step happens on their phone, takes under 3 minutes, and feels natural — not like filling a form.

## What's Included

### 1. Brand Home Page
A polished landing page at your main website address. Tells your brand story, showcases your makhanas, and directs visitors to WhatsApp to place an order. This one is a collaborative effort — your vision, references, and inputs will shape how it looks and feels.

### 2. Campaign Page (QR destination)
The page customers land on after scanning the QR code. Walks them through the free drink offer, collects their details, generates their unique redemption code, and ends with the makhana discount and WhatsApp order button.

Each cafe partner gets their own unique QR code — so you always know which cafe is bringing in the most customers.

### 3. Admin Dashboard (private, password-protected)
A simple control panel for you. No technical knowledge needed.

**What you can do from the dashboard:**
- See every person who signed up — name, WhatsApp number, which cafe, date and time
- Read all feedback and ratings submitted
- View a quick summary — total signups, total feedback, which cafe is performing best
- Add new cafe partners and instantly download their unique QR code
- Export your full customer list as a spreadsheet anytime

### 4. Secure Data Storage
All customer information is stored securely in a private database. No data is shared with anyone.

## What We'll Collect From Customers

Below is the information the website will gather at each step. These are my suggestions — happy to add, remove, or adjust any of these based on what matters most to you.

**When they sign up (before the drink):**
- Name
- WhatsApp number

**After they try the drink (feedback form):**
- Taste rating (1 to 5 stars)
- Flavor preference — sweet / tangy / earthy / less sweet
- Age group — 18–24 / 25–34 / 35–44 / 45+
- Would they try it again — yes / maybe / no
- Email address (optional)
- Any other comments (optional)

*Behind the scenes, we also quietly track which cafe the signup came from — this is embedded in each cafe's unique QR code and requires no action from the customer.*
