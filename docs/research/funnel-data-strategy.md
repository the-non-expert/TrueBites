# TrueBites Funnel — Data Strategy & Research Notes

## Products
- **Primary:** Roasted Makhanas (year-round, the conversion target)
- **Bait:** Sattu-based cool drink (seasonal, summer, free sample trigger)

## Funnel Overview
QR at cafe table → Mobile landing page → Data collection → Free sattu drink redemption → Feedback on drink → 30% off on makhanas (positioned as "try our next product")

---

## Data Collection Strategy

### Pre-drink form (keep SHORT — 2 fields max)
- Name
- WhatsApp number *(primary retention channel — 90%+ open rates in India vs 20% email)*

### Post-drink feedback form (they're warm now — can ask more)
- Star rating on the sattu drink (1–5)
- Flavor preference: sweet / tangy / earthy / less sweet *(free R&D for future variants)*
- Open feedback field (optional)
- Age bracket: 18-24 / 25-34 / 35-44 / 45+ *(2-second dropdown, tells you real demographic)*
- Health-conscious self-identification: Yes / Somewhat / Not really *(audience segmentation for future messaging)*
- Email *(collect here, not upfront — they're more willing after getting value)*

### Embedded in QR (invisible to user, critical for analytics)
- **Cafe ID** — every cafe partner gets a unique QR code. Tracks which location drives best leads.
  - Tells you: which neighborhood converts, which cafe partner to invest more in, geo-intelligence for free

---

## Fake Number Risk
- No OTP to avoid drop-off and capital cost — accepted trade-off at early stage
- Mitigation: drink redemption requires showing the confirmation screen (with name visible) to cafe staff
- Natural filter: staff won't serve someone whose screen name doesn't match vibe / doesn't feel real
- Accept ~20-30% fake numbers as cost of low-friction early acquisition

---

## Retention & Expansion Channels

### WhatsApp (priority channel)
- Post-feedback: send WhatsApp message with 30% off makhana code
- 7-day follow-up: "Did you try our makhanas?"
- New product drops, flavor launches, seasonal offers
- Build a broadcast list — this IS the business at early stage

### Referral Loop (not in v1 but plan for v2)
- "Share with one friend → both get 15% off first makhana order"
- Turns each cafe customer into a micro-distributor
- Cost: marginal. Upside: exponential.

### Pre-launch e-commerce list
- Every signup = warm lead for DTC store launch
- 500 verified WhatsApp numbers before Shopify launch = real traction

---

## Per-Cafe Analytics (requires unique QR per cafe)
- Signups per cafe location
- Conversion rate (signed up → completed feedback)
- Best-performing neighborhoods
- Feedback score variance by location (product consistency check)
- Data to double down on high-performing cafe partners

---

## Traction Story (for investors/buyers)
- "X people tasted our sattu drink across Y cafes in [city]"
- "Z% gave 4+ star rating"
- "N% went on to claim makhana discount"
- The data collected here IS the pitch deck proof point

---

## Seasonality Note
- Sattu drink = summer play (~March–June)
- Funnel must convert to makhana buyers before summer ends
- After summer: pivot QR campaign to makhana sampling directly, sattu as memory/brand recall

---

## Framing That Works
- Sattu drink offer: "Try something new, on us"
- Makhana offer: "Try our next product at 30% off" *(not just a discount — feels like an invitation)*
- Brand through-line: both are TrueBites — healthy Indian foods, real ingredients

---

## Order & Redemption Mechanism
- **Store:** WhatsApp (primary, for now — no e-commerce needed in v1)
- **30% off redemption:** User gets a unique discount code on feedback completion (e.g., TBFAN-2847), screenshots it, sends on WhatsApp to order makhanas
- **Verification:** Operator cross-checks name + number against collected list OR accepts code as-is
- **CTA post-feedback:** Pre-filled WhatsApp link — "Hi, I'd like to order makhanas. My code is TBFAN-2847"
- Discount code approach is cleaner than name+number lookup for the operator
